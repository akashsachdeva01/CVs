# Network Optimization Engineer – Nokia (UK, Hybrid)

## Overview
As a Network Optimization Engineer at Nokia, you'll enhance mature wireless networks across Europe alongside a dynamic team of experts. Your focus will be on end-to-end optimization, including capacity, architecture, and performance analysis.

You will collaborate closely with teams, conduct root cause analyses, and implement best practices post-launch to meet evolving customer needs. You will also engage directly with customers, providing consultative insights on 4G and 5G technologies.

---

## Responsibilities

- Lead end-to-end optimization of mature wireless networks to enhance capacity and architecture across Europe
- Conduct root cause analyses to identify and resolve performance issues
- Ensure seamless post-launch operations and continuous improvement
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to promote knowledge sharing and best practices
- Engage directly with customers, providing insights on 4G and 5G technologies
- Utilize advanced tools and methodologies to solve complex network performance challenges
- Drive customer satisfaction by improving network performance and service quality
- Contribute to strategic initiatives supporting Nokia’s leadership in network services
- Thrive in a fast-paced, innovative environment with continuous learning opportunities

---

## Required Skills & Experience

- Strong understanding of:
  - 5G FR1 (NSA/SA) and FR2
  - LTE architecture
- Knowledge of Nokia RRM, features, and parameters
- Experience with TCP packet analysis using Wireshark (or similar tools)
- Ability to analyze:
  - Nokia internal syslogs
  - eNB/gNB logs
- Understanding of benchmarking methodologies
- Proven experience in 5G & LTE end-to-end optimization
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills
- Ability to work collaboratively in a team environment
- Willingness to learn and adapt to evolving technologies

---

## Preferred (Nice-to-Have) Skills

- Experience with:
  - EMIL traces

  - Logging tools (QXDM, XCAL, etc.)
- Familiarity with tools:
  - ATOLL
  - RNP9955
  - ACTIX
  - NEMO
  - LTE PAT
- Knowledge of national benchmarking methodologies (e.g., Umlaut, ARCEP)
- Nokia Certified Solutions Specialist (5G Radio Network Optimization)
- Experience with ORAN / VRAN
- Multi-vendor experience (Huawei, Ericsson, ZTE) including:
  - Network swaps
  - KPI mapping
  - Feature mapping
