## The Role

The successful individual will design, implement, and support business-critical **Operational Technology telecoms systems** necessary to monitor and control plant and safety systems used in the **SSE Distribution Control Centre**.

---

## Responsibilities

- Producing standards, specifications, and target architectures  
- Creating custom tools to aid in the design and delivery of standards and architectures  
- Translating business requirements into practical telecoms deliverables  
- Providing SME guidance on telecoms infrastructure including:
  - Radio  
  - Fibre  
  - SDH  
  - Data networks  

---

## Requirements

- Strong knowledge and experience in the operation and design of data networks  
- Proven experience in planning and deploying data networks  
- Demonstrable knowledge of:
  - IP  
  - Ethernet  
  - Wireless technologies  
- Understanding of telecom infrastructure technologies, including:
  - Fibre optic cabling  
  - SDH/PDH multiplexers  
  - Microwave radio  
  - Ancillary equipment (space and power)  
- Understanding of **SCADA networks** and their integration with data networking
