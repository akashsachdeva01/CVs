## Why this job matters
We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced **Senior Network Engineer** to lead the design, implementation, and support of enterprise-scale network infrastructure.

This role blends:
- Hands-on engineering  
- Strategic design  
- Operational delivery  

You will work across **on-prem and cloud-based environments**, architecting **secure, scalable, and resilient solutions**, while collaborating with cross-functional teams.

---

## What you’ll be doing

- Design, configure, and maintain enterprise-scale **LAN, WAN, and hybrid cloud networks (AWS/Azure)**
- Implement and manage network services:
  - VPNs
  - VLANs
  - QoS
  - DNS
  - DHCP
  - ACI
  - EVPN
  - Network segmentation
- Deploy and support:
  - Firewalls
  - Routers
  - Switches
  - Load balancers
- Collaborate with security teams to enforce access controls and compliance
- Troubleshoot complex issues and perform **root cause analysis**
- Monitor performance, perform **capacity planning**, and optimize reliability
- Document:
  - Network architecture
  - Configurations
  - Failover strategies
- Ensure alignment with:
  - IT policies
  - Audit requirements
  - Industry best practices
- Stay updated with emerging technologies (reading, briefings, talks)
